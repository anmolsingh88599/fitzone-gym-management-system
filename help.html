Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a new member?

Open the Add section, enter the member details and save the membership.

How can I record a payment?

Open Payments and select Add Payment to enter the member's payment details.

How do expiry reminders work?

FitZone can show members whose memberships are about to expire.

Can I change my gym information?

Yes. Open More → Gym Profile to update your gym information.
